When Should Children Start Learning the Quran?

Starting Quran education at an early age helps children build confident pronunciation and a lifelong connection with the Holy Quran. Many educators consider the early primary years a valuable window for language and memorisation development, when young minds are especially receptive to rhythm and repetition.

At Quranly Hub, we turn Quran learning into an engaging, interactive and joyful experience through modern digital tools and personalised 1-on-1 attention.
