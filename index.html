Why QuranlyHub

Why families choose QuranlyHub for online Quran classes

Most parents come to us after a group class stopped working — the child fell behind, the timing clashed with school, or nobody could say what progress had been made. Every part of QuranlyHub is built to fix exactly that.

True one-to-one attention

Every class is a private lesson between one student and one teacher. Your teacher hears each letter, corrects makharij on the spot, and adapts the pace instead of moving the whole class along.

Certified, verified teachers

Tutors hold an ijazah or a recognised Islamic studies degree, are tested on tajweed and spoken English before hiring, and are background-verified. You can request a male or female teacher.

Timings that fit real life

Classes run seven days a week in your local time zone — before school, after work, late evening or weekends. Reschedule with notice at no cost, and pause for holidays or exams.

Progress you can actually see

Parents receive a written monthly report covering lessons completed, tajweed rules mastered, memorisation retained and what to practise at home. No guessing whether it's working.

Built for children's attention spans

Kids' lessons run 30 focused minutes with screen-shared Qaida pages, repetition games and short goals. Teachers are trained specifically for children aged 4 to 14.

Patient with adults and reverts

Starting at the alphabet as an adult takes courage. Adult and revert students learn privately, at their own speed, with teachers who have taught hundreds of beginners before.

Responsive human support

Message admissions on WhatsApp and reach a person, not a bot. Teacher changes, timetable moves and billing questions are handled the same day.

Honest, simple pricing

One monthly fee, no registration charge, no hidden costs and no long contract. Sibling discounts apply automatically, and you can cancel whenever you need to.

HD classes on any device

Join from a phone, tablet or laptop over Zoom, Google Meet or Skype. Teachers share a digital mushaf or Qaida on screen, so you don't need to buy anything to begin.

How we select and monitor our teachers

  1. Credential check. Ijazah, degree or madrasah certification is verified before an interview is offered.
  2. Live recitation test. Applicants recite for a senior tutor who assesses makharij, tajweed and fluency.
  3. Teaching demo. A mock lesson with a real student measures patience, clarity and pacing.
  4. Background verification. Identity and references are checked before any class with a child.
  5. Ongoing review. Lessons are sampled each term and parent feedback feeds directly into retention.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions about online Quran classes

Everything parents and students ask us before booking a first class.

What is QuranlyHub?

QuranlyHub is an online Quran academy that provides live one-to-one Quran classes over video call. Certified male and female teachers teach Noorani Qaida, Quran reading, tajweed, hifz, translation, tafsir and basic Islamic studies to children, adults and reverts in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait and Saudi Arabia. Classes are taught in English, with Arabic and Urdu support available.

How much do online Quran classes cost at QuranlyHub?

Monthly plans start at $39 for eight 30-minute one-to-one classes. Three days a week (12 classes) is $55 and five days a week (20 classes) is $79. Hifz and 60-minute plans are quoted individually. There is no registration fee, families receive a sibling discount, and the first trial class is free. See full pricing.

Is the trial class really free?

Yes — the first class is completely free and no card details are required. The trial is a full one-to-one lesson in which the teacher assesses the student's current level and recommends a study plan. You only pay if you decide to continue, and there is no pressure if you don't.

Are online Quran classes effective for children?

Yes — one-to-one online classes are usually more effective for children than group classes. The teacher hears every letter the child pronounces and corrects mistakes immediately, which is impossible when one teacher is supervising fifteen students. Lessons are kept to 30 minutes to match a child's attention span, run at the same time each week to build routine, and parents receive written monthly progress reports. More on kids' Quran classes.

Do you have female Quran teachers for sisters and girls?

Yes — QuranlyHub has qualified female Quran teachers who teach girls, sisters and mothers. Choose a female or male teacher when you book, and that preference is honoured for every class, including any future teacher change. Meet our female teachers.

What do I need to join an online Quran class?

A smartphone, tablet or computer, a stable internet connection and a headset. Classes run on Zoom, Google Meet or Skype, and the teacher shares a digital mushaf or Qaida on screen — so you don't need to buy books or install special software to start.

Can I choose my own class timings across different time zones?

Yes — classes are scheduled in your local time zone, seven days a week. Early-morning, after-school, late-evening and weekend slots are all available, with teachers covering GMT, EST, PST, AEST and GST. You can reschedule with reasonable notice at no cost.

Are your Quran teachers qualified?

Every teacher holds an ijazah or a recognised Islamic studies qualification and is background-verified. Applicants are tested on tajweed and spoken English, deliver a live teaching demo before hiring, and have lessons sampled throughout the year. Read about our hiring standards.

How long does it take to learn to read the Quran?

A complete beginner taking three classes a week typically finishes Noorani Qaida in three to six months. Reading fluently from the mushaf with applied tajweed usually takes a further twelve to eighteen months. The biggest variables are age, class frequency and ten minutes of daily practice at home.

Which countries does QuranlyHub serve?

The UK, USA, Canada, Australia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait and Saudi Arabia are our core countries, and we accept students elsewhere when the timetable allows. Classes are taught in English, with Arabic and Urdu support on request.

Can adults and reverts start from the very beginning?

Yes — adults and reverts start on a private beginner track that begins at the Arabic alphabet. There is no group class and no comparison with other students. Teachers experienced with adult learners cover reading alongside salah, wudu and daily duas. More on adult Quran classes.

What happens if I miss a class?

Tell your teacher or admissions on WhatsApp before the lesson and it is rescheduled free within the same month. Classes missed without notice aren't carried forward. You can also pause your plan entirely for holidays or exams by letting us know in advance.
